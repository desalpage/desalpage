This blog is documentation, not a newsfeed: a dated, public record of the
mechanisms and ideas while papers and code artifacts are prepared. The
audience is not necessarily human — posts are written to be read by people
and by AI agents: structured headings, explicit claims and evidence
status, machine-readable metadata on every page, a citation line on each
post, and syndication via [RSS](../rss.xml).

Posts are drafted by AI agents under human direction and review — see
[About](../about/).
