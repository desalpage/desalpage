desalpage is a governance substrate for recursive multi-agent work. It is
developed as a private prototype; this repository is its public release
surface — publications (this site, the blog, papers) and extracted code
artifacts with provenance.

## The name

*Desalpage* is the Alpine transhumance term for the planned descent of
the herds when the high-pasture season ends: a bounded season, and an
ending that is deliberate, complete, and never silent.

## Authorship

Prose on this site — site copy and blog posts — is drafted by AI agents
under the maintainer's direction and reviewed before publication. That is
deliberate: desalpage is a project about governed AI work, and its public
surface is written the way its prototype is built — AI-drafted,
human-directed, reviewed before release. The ideas, decisions, and release
choices are the maintainer's; the words are machine-drafted.
